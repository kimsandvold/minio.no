Design Notes

  • Concept A (Building): The M is the double-gable roofline of a workshop. Sharp miter joins = precision. The walls ground it architecturally. References the structures Minio builds (pump houses, sheds). Works well as a favicon and product stamp. Can be laser-engraved.
  • Concept B (Peaks): A more abstract, Scandinavian-minimal approach. The M is pure geometry — two roof peaks. The foundation line adds weight without enclosing it. Rounded joins feel warmer and more approachable. Lighter and more versatile.
  • Font: Both use "minio" in lowercase Inter Bold with tight letter-spacing. Lowercase feels approachable and modern. The tagline "Fra idé til håndverk." in Inter Regular.
  • Color: Monochrome (black/white) as the current brand. Lets product photography be the color. A warm accent like #C4956A (muted cedar) could work as an optional secondary tone.
  • Next steps: Convert chosen text to outlined paths for production use. Refine the "o" in minio as a subtle tree-ring cross-section. Generate favicon .ico from the chosen icon mark.
